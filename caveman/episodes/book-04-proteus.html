Old Man of Sea

Book IV, Part 2 — Morning. Menelaos tells how he found out Odysseus was alive.

Telemakos morning, before they leave

Menelaos, son of Atreus. Now we talk about father. Tell me — do you know anything? Tell me true thing. Father alive? Father dead? Do not make soft story. Tell true thing.

Menelaos

You ask right. I will tell true thing. I learned it from old man who cannot lie — because he is god of sea itself, Proteus, Old Man of Sea. I will tell you how I caught him and what he said.

I was stuck in Egypt. No wind. Ships could not move. Days passed. Weeks passed. Men were starving. Then daughter of Proteus found me on beach. She took pity. She said: catch my father. He comes out of sea at noon to sleep with his seals on shore. He will try to escape you — he changes shape. He becomes lion. He becomes serpent. He becomes water. He becomes tree. Hold on. Do not let go. When he stops changing — he is tired. Then he must answer your questions.

Telemakos

You held on?

Menelaos

We held on. Lion. Serpent. Leopard. Boar. Running water. Tall tree. We held on. He stopped. He became old man again. He was angry. He said: who told you to do this? I told him. He said fine. Ask your questions.

I asked about my men. Who died on way home. He told me. I asked about Agamemnon my brother. He told me — Aigisthos killed him, your wife helped, Orestes will avenge him. Then I asked: is there anyone else still alive, still stuck somewhere, not home yet?

Telemakos very still

What did he say.

Menelaos

He said: son of Laertes. Odysseus. I saw him on island of nymph Kalypso. He was crying. Sitting on shore, looking at sea, crying. He has no ship. He has no crew. He cannot get home. Kalypso keeps him there. That is what Proteus said.

Telemakos

Alive. He is alive.

Menelaos

Alive. On island. Crying. Wanting to come home. That is what I know. Now — stay here. Stay ten days. Twelve days. I will give you gifts, good horses, fine things for journey.

Telemakos

Menelaos. Thank you. But I cannot stay. Men at home are eating father's animals, drinking father's wine. Every day I am gone is one more day they take. Give me gift I can carry on ship. Horses I cannot take to Ithaka — Ithaka is small island, no room to run horses. Keep horses. Give me something else.

Menelaos

Good answer. Right answer. You are son of Odysseus. He would have said same thing. Take this silver bowl. Take this mixing jar — pure gold, made by Hephaistos himself, given to me by king of Sidon. Take these. Go home. Father is coming.

They embrace. Telemakos and Peisistratos take road back to Pylos, back to ship, back to sea.
Homer Very Small — after Homer — Ralph Hutchison
