Homer Very Small: Old King Nestor

Book III — a puppet script — after Homer

Puppets Needed

  • TELEMAKOS — braver now, but still nervous around important people
  • ATHENA / MENTOR — same disguise as Book II; becomes an eagle at the end
  • NESTOR — very old king, slow, enormous white beard, tells long stories
  • PEISISTRATOS — Nestor's son, young, cheerful, slightly in awe of Telemakos
  • EAGLE — cloth or paper on a stick; swoops up and away at the end
Scene 1 — The Beach at Pylos
Big beach. Many men, many fires, much smoke. Telemakos and Athena/Mentor arrive.
TELEMAKOSMany men. Telemakos not know what to say. That man very old. Very important. How Telemakos talk to important old man?
ATHENA / MENTORTelemakos. Go. Say words. Gods help brave man. I am here.
Peisistratos runs over.
PEISISTRATOSStrangers! Welcome! Come eat! Eat first. Talk after. This is how we do.
Scene 2 — Dinner with Nestor
They eat. Finally Nestor looks at Telemakos.
NESTORGood. Now. Who are you. Where you from. Why you come.
TELEMAKOSOld king. I am Telemakos. Father is Odysseus. You fight together at Troy. Father not come home. Long time. You hear anything? Father alive? Father dead? Tell me true thing.
NESTORAh. (pause) You look like him. Same face. Good.
NESTORTroy: ten year war. Finally over. Greeks go home. But Greeks fight each other after. Half go one way. Half go other. Nestor come home. Odysseus turn boat around, go back. After that: Nestor not know.
NESTORYou hear about Agamemnon? Big king. Come home. Wife's boyfriend kill him. Very bad. But — son named Orestes. Orestes grow up. Orestes avenge father. (looks directly at Telemakos) Good son do that.
TELEMAKOS...Yes.
NESTORNestor not know where Odysseus is. But — go to Sparta. Ask king Menelaos. Menelaos know more. My son Peisistratos go with you.
PEISISTRATOSYes. I go. Is fine.
Scene 3 — The Eagle
Night. Big fire. Everyone gathered. Athena is standing there, looking like Mentor. Then — she transforms. Eagle puppet swoops up and away into the dark.
Everyone stares. Long silence.
NESTOR...That was a god. That was a god walking with you the whole time and nobody say anything.
NESTORTelemakos. The gods like you very much. (to everyone) Somebody get more bulls. Right now. Tonight.
PEISISTRATOS(quietly to Telemakos) Does this happen to you a lot?
TELEMAKOSNo. First time.
Morning. Gifts. Chariot. They leave for Sparta. Nestor watches from the shore.

Puppet Show Tips

  • Nestor talks slowly and takes up space. Let his pauses breathe. The "Ah." before he answers Telemakos should be long enough to make the audience wonder what he'll say.
  • The Orestes story is Nestor's hint to Telemakos: avenge your father. Nestor doesn't say it directly — he tells a story. Play it like he's watching Telemakos's face the whole time.
  • The eagle reveal: swap the Mentor puppet offstage for the eagle puppet in one fluid motion. Speed matters — the faster the swap, the more magical.
  • Peisistratos's "Does this happen to you a lot?" is the laugh line of the episode. Deliver it deadpan.
  • Nestor ordering more bulls immediately after the god reveals herself is very funny and very true to character. Play it straight — he is not joking.
