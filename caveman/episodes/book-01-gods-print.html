Homer Very Small: The Gods Argue

Book I, Part 1 — a puppet script — after Homer

Puppets Needed

  • ZEUS — king of gods, big, slow, usually eating
  • ATHENA — goddess, quick, sharp, always has a plan
Scene — The Mountain
Very tall mountain. Clouds. Zeus eating. Athena walks up.
ATHENAFather. We talk about Odysseus?
ZEUSOdysseus. Yes. Odysseus stuck. Island. Woman keep him. Name: Calypso. Calypso very pretty. Odysseus want go home. Calypso not let go.
ATHENAOdysseus good man. Odysseus pray to us. Odysseus give gifts. We help other men come home from Troy. We not help Odysseus. Why not?
ZEUSPoseidon. Poseidon very mad at Odysseus. Odysseus hurt Poseidon son. Big son. One eye. Cyclops. Poseidon make sea very hard for Odysseus.
ATHENABut Poseidon not here right now. Poseidon at party. Very far away. We decide now, before Poseidon come back. Send Odysseus home.
ZEUSFine. Yes. Good. We send Hermes to island. Hermes tell Calypso: time to let him go. You — go to Ithaca. Help the boy. Make him brave.
ATHENAYes. I go now.
Athena flies off the mountain, very fast, toward the sea.

Puppet Show Tips

  • This is a short scene — good opener. Sets up everything. Keep it brisk.
  • Zeus should move slowly and talk with full mouth. Athena moves fast and decisive.
  • The Cyclops can get a big reaction if you pause after "One eye" and let kids guess who that is.
  • Athena flying off: swoop the puppet up and away quickly. The speed says everything about her character.
