The Island Council

Book II — Telemakos asks for help. Nobody helps.

Telemakos son of Odysseus

Men. Sit. Listen.

Father gone. Long time. Father not dead. Father not home. Bad men come to house. Bad men eat food. All food. Every day food. Telemakos say go. Bad men not go.

Men here! Old men! Do something!

Telemakos sits down. Cries a little.
Antinoos suitor, very confident

Telemakos, no. Not our fault. Listen.

Mother trick. Mother say: wait. Antinoos wait. Telemakos old man need cloth first. Cloth finish, then I pick husband. Mother make cloth. Day: make cloth. Night: untake cloth. Three year! We wait three year! Servant tell us. We find out. Mother bad.

Send mother back to father-man. Father-man pick husband. Then we go. Simple.

Telemakos

No. Telemakos not push out mother. Mother make Telemakos. Also: father-man maybe not want mother back. Telemakos pay much goat.

You go. You go from house. You go.

Gods watching.

* * *
Eurymakhos another suitor

Telemakos wrong. Also: Antinoos right. Mother trick us. We not go until mother pick husband. Very simple. Assembly over.

Telemakos

Fine. Nobody help. Fine.

Telemakos ask one thing. Give boat. Give twenty man. Telemakos go find father. Go ask king Nestor. Go ask king Menelaos. Maybe father alive. Maybe father dead. Telemakos need know.

Mentor old man, very upset

Men! Men sit here do nothing! Odysseus good king. Odysseus good to you. Now son ask for help. Nobody help. Suitor men eat all food. Nobody stop. Shame! Big shame on all of you!

Mentor sits down, grumbling.
Leokritus another suitor, waves hand

Mentor old. Mentor wrong. Even if Odysseus come back — suitor men very many. Odysseus one man. Odysseus lose. Assembly over. Everybody go home. Telemakhos: good luck with boat.

Two big eagles fly over very fast. They fight each other. They scratch the faces of the men below. They fly away.
Halitherses very old man, stands up fast

Wait wait wait. Eagles! Eagles mean Odysseus! Odysseus come back! Soon! Telemakhos — father coming! Suitor men — stop eating! Very bad thing happen to you!

Eurymakhos

Old man! Old man sit down! Bird just bird. Odysseus not come. Odysseus dead long time. Halitherses wrong always.

Also: your mother — send back to father-man. We keep eating until then.

Assembly over. Everybody leave. Nothing happen. Same as before.
* * *
Telemakos walks alone to the beach. Sits by the water.
Telemakos alone

Gods. Help.

A stranger walks up. The stranger looks like Mentor. But the walk is different. The eyes are very bright.
Athena disguised as Mentor

Telemakos. Why sit? Father gone long time. Strong man not sit. Strong man go find.

Telemakos

Stranger, yes, but — boat. Need boat. Old men not give boat. Suitor men very many. Telemakos small.

Athena

Boat coming. Telemakos not worry about boat. Also: suitor men not as scary as they look. Trust me.

Go home now. Pack food. Pack warm thing. Come back tonight. Boat here. We go.

Telemakos feels different. Stands straighter. Walks back to the house. The stranger is gone. Just gone.
Homer Very Small — after Homer — Ralph Hutchison
