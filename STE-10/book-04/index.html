Book IV

Sparta — Gold everywhere, grief underneath. Two war stories. The man on the island.

Chapter 1

The Palace of Menelaos

  • 1.1The Double Wedding Book IV, lines 1–67 — A feast is in progress. Eteoneos asks whether to let the strangers in.
  • 1.2The Palace Book IV, lines 68–167 — Gold everywhere. Silver. Ivory. Menelaos speaks of grief.
  • 1.3Helen Comes Down Book IV, lines 120–167 — She knows who Telemakhos is before anyone tells her.

Chapter 2

Helen's Table

  • 2.1The Drug Book IV, lines 219–264 — Helen puts something in the wine. The grief lifts. She explains what it does.
  • 2.2Helen's Story Book IV, lines 240–264 — Odysseus walked into Troy alone, in disguise, and she knew him.
  • 2.3Menelaos's Story Book IV, lines 265–289 — The wooden horse. Helen called the Greeks' wives' voices. Odysseus held them still.

Chapter 3

Proteus

  • 3.1What Menelaos Heard Book IV, lines 290–425 — Morning. Telemakhos asks directly. Menelaos tells how he found out.
  • 3.2The Old Man of the Sea Book IV, lines 425–570 — The ambush, the shapeshifting, the holding on.
  • 3.3What Proteus Said Book IV, lines 555–592 — Odysseus is alive. He is on an island. He is alone.

Chapter 4

Back in Ithaka

  • 4.1The Suitors Learn Book IV, lines 625–674 — Antinoos finds out Telemakhos sailed. They plan an ambush.
  • 4.2Penelope Book IV, lines 675–767 — She learns her son is gone. She sits on the floor and weeps.
  • 4.3The Dream Book IV, lines 787–end — Athena sends a phantom to Penelope. It cannot say if Telemakhos will live.
