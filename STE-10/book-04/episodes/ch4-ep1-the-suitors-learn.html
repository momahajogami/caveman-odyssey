Chapter 4 · Episode 4.1

The Suitors Learn

Book IV, lines 625–674 — Antinoos finds out Telemakhos sailed. They plan an ambush.

Back in Ithaka, the suitors find out.

A herald brings the news to Antinoos — Telemakhos is gone. He left in the night with twenty men, borrowed a ship, sailed without telling anyone. By the time anyone noticed he was missing, the ship was already out of sight.

Antinoos sits very still for a moment. The feast around him goes on — people eating, drinking, talking — and he sits at the center of it and says nothing.

Then he stands. "This is bad," he says. "We underestimated him. I underestimated him. I thought he was nothing and he sailed to Pylos and Sparta — to Nestor, to Menelaos — looking for news of his father. Looking for allies." He looks around at the others. "If Odysseus is alive, and if Telemakhos finds enough people who will act for him, the situation here becomes difficult."

Eurymakhos listens with his measuring eyes.

They agree: Telemakhos cannot be allowed to come back. They will take a ship and wait at the straits between Ithaka and Samos — a narrow passage he will have to come through. They will kill him at sea. It will look like an accident. It will look like bad luck at sea.

They plan it calmly, like men who have made plans before.

One of them goes to find a ship.
