Book III

Pylos — Telemakhos meets old Nestor and learns what it means to be a son.

Chapter 1

The Shore at Pylos

  • 1.1Pylos Book III, lines 1–35 — The ship arrives at dawn. Four thousand men are sacrificing on the beach.
  • 1.2Go and Ask Book III, lines 36–101 — Telemakhos is afraid to approach. Mentor pushes. Peisistratos comes to meet them.
  • 1.3Who Are You? Book III, lines 102–129 — Nestor asks. Telemakhos answers. The conversation begins.

Chapter 2

What Nestor Knows

  • 2.1After Troy Book III, lines 130–200 — Nestor tells what happened when the Greeks tried to go home.
  • 2.2Agamemnon Book III, lines 193–316 — The murder, the revenge, and what Nestor is really saying to Telemakhos.
  • 2.3No News Book III, lines 184–252 — Telemakhos asks directly. Nestor has nothing. Go to Menelaos.
  • 2.4Stay the Night Book III, lines 317–403 — Nestor insists. Mentor goes to the ship. Athena flies away as a bird.

Chapter 3

Morning and the Road

  • 3.1The Bird Book III, lines 371–403 — Athena is revealed. Nestor makes offerings. The night in Pylos.
  • 3.2Polykaste Book III, lines 404–468 — Morning sacrifice. Nestor's daughter bathes Telemakhos. He is made ready.
  • 3.3The Chariot Book III, lines 469–end — Telemakhos and Peisistratos ride toward Sparta. The first day on the road.
