Chapter 1 · Episode 1.1

Pylos

Book III, lines 1–35 — The ship arrives at dawn. Four thousand men are sacrificing on the beach.

They sail through the night and the dawn finds them at sea.

The sun comes up out of the water — bronze and enormous, turning the surface to hammered metal — and ahead of them is Pylos, and the shore at Pylos. As they come in close they can see something that stops the whole crew.

The beach is covered with people. Nine groups of men, five hundred in each group, and each group has nine black bulls they are killing in sacrifice to Poseidon. The thighbones are burning. The fat is smoking. The smell of it reaches the ship before they reach the shore — blood and fire and the sea.

Four thousand five hundred men on the sand, gathered around their fires in the morning light.

Telemakhos stands at the prow and looks at it all. He is from a small island. He has never seen anything like this.
