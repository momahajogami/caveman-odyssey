Book I

Ithaka — The night everything changes for Telemakhos.

Chapter 1

The Singer Calls

  • 1.1The Muse Book I, lines 1–21 — Before the story, the singer asks for help.
  • 1.2The Gods Decide Book I, lines 22–95 — On Olympos, while Poseidon is away, Athena makes her case.

Chapter 2

The Hall of the Suitors

  • 2.1Mentes at the Gate Book I, lines 96–155 — Athena arrives in Ithaka. Telemakhos is the first to see her.
  • 2.2What the Boy Carries Book I, lines 156–251 — Telemakhos tells the stranger his situation.
  • 2.3The Bard Sings Book I, lines 319–364 — Penelope comes downstairs. Telemakhos finds his voice.

Chapter 3

The Stranger's Words

  • 3.1What Mentes Knows Book I, lines 252–295 — The stranger tells Telemakhos what he knows of his father.
  • 3.2The Instructions Book I, lines 295–318 — Mentes tells Telemakhos exactly what to do next.
  • 3.3Antinoos and Eurymakhos Book I, lines 365–420 — The suitors notice that Telemakhos is different tonight.

Chapter 4

After Dark

  • 4.1The Stranger Gone Book I, lines 421–444 — Mentes leaves. Telemakhos stands at the gate.
  • 4.2Eurykleia Book I, lines 428–444 — The nurse takes Telemakhos to bed. The first day ends.
