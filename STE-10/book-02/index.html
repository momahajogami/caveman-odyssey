Book II

Ithaka — Telemakhos speaks in public, gets nowhere, and leaves in the dark.

Chapter 1

The Assembly

  • 1.1Morning Book II, lines 1–14 — Telemakhos wakes and walks to the assembly ground.
  • 1.2Telemakhos Speaks Book II, lines 15–79 — His first public speech. He weeps. He throws the scepter down.
  • 1.3Antinoos Answers Book II, lines 80–145 — The suitors blame Penelope. Telemakhos will not give her up.
  • 1.4The Eagles Book II, lines 146–207 — An omen arrives. Halitherses reads it. Eurymakhos refuses it.
  • 1.5The Assembly Ends Book II, lines 208–256 — Mentor speaks. Nobody listens. Telemakhos walks to the shore alone.

Chapter 2

The Ship

  • 2.1Athena on the Shore Book II, lines 257–336 — Mentor finds Telemakhos at the water. Stop grieving. Start moving.
  • 2.2The Storeroom Book II, lines 337–381 — Back at the palace. Telemakhos loads provisions in secret.
  • 2.3The Oath Book II, lines 382–434 — Eurykleia weeps. Telemakhos swears her to twelve days of silence.

Chapter 3

The Night

  • 3.1The Crew Book II, lines 386–434 — Mentor moves through the town. Twenty men come to the harbor.
  • 3.2The Dark Departure Book II, lines 435–end — Sleep on the suitors. Telemakhos to the ship. The wind comes.
