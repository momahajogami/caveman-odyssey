Chapter 2 · Episode 2.1

Athena on the Shore

Book II, lines 257–336 — Mentor finds Telemakhos at the water. Stop grieving. Start moving.

Mentor finds him there.

He comes down the path to the shore and stands beside Telemakhos at the water's edge. He is an old man, gray, weathered — or appears to be.

"Your father would be ashamed," Mentor says. It is not unkind. It is simply true, and they both know it. "Not of the situation — of sitting here. You are not made of worse material than he was. You have his hands, his eyes, his way of holding still. Stop grieving and start moving."

Telemakhos turns to look at him.

"You called the assembly," Mentor says. "That was the right thing. They refused you — also the right thing to know. Now you have the information. What you need next is a ship and twenty men and two days' provisions." He pauses. "I will get you the ship and the crew. You go home and prepare the stores. Tell no one. Have everything ready and come to the harbor tonight after dark."

Telemakhos stares at him for a moment. He thinks of the stranger who came to the hall — also a sea-captain, also a friend of his father's, also gone without a trace. He does not let himself think the thought all the way through.

"I'll be there," he says.

He turns from the water and walks back toward the palace. His step is different from the step that brought him here.

Behind him, Mentor is already gone.
