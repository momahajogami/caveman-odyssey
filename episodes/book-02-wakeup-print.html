Caveman Odyssey: Boy Wake Up

Book II, Part 1 — a puppet script — after Homer

Puppets Needed

  • TELEMAKOS — carries a spear today; moves differently than before
  • AIGYPTIOS — very old man, first elder to speak at assembly
  • SERVANTS — one or two background puppets who notice the change
Scene — The Morning
First light. The house is quiet. Telemakos wakes. He gets up fast. Good clothes. Good sandals. Spear. He walks out.
Two servants watch him pass. They look at each other.
TELEMAKOS(to himself, walking) Today. Today Telemakos say it out loud.
He walks to the assembly ground. Sits in his father's seat. The old men look up.
AIGYPTIOSWho called this assembly? Long time since we have assembly. Who called? Why?
TELEMAKOSI called. I have something to say.
He takes the speaker's staff. Everyone waits. He takes a breath.
[continue into The Island Council]

Puppet Show Tips

  • This is a short scene — a bridge. Its whole job is to show that Telemakos is different this morning. Move him faster, hold him straighter than before.
  • The servants noticing: just a glance between them, no words. The audience reads it.
  • "Today Telemakos say it out loud." — deliver quietly, to himself. This is not a speech. It is a decision.
  • End on the held breath before he speaks to the assembly. Then go straight into the next episode.
