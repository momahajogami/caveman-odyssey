Old King Nestor

Book III — Telemakos sails to Pylos. The old king tells a very long story.

Big beach at Pylos. Nine groups of men. Each group: very many men. Each group: nine black bulls. Much smoke. Much fire. Much smell. Telemakos and Athena (still disguised as Mentor) arrive by boat.
Telemakos quietly, nervous

Many men. Telemakos not know what to say. That man very old. Very important. How Telemakos talk to important old man?

Athena

Telemakos. Go. Say words. Gods help brave man. Also: Telemakos not alone. I here.

Nestor's son Peisistratos sees them. Runs over. Very friendly.
Peisistratos Nestor's son, young, cheerful

Strangers! Welcome! Come eat! Eat first. Talk after. This is how we do.

They eat. Big feast. Finally Nestor looks at Telemakos.
Nestor very old king, careful with words

Good. Now. Who are you. Where you from. Why you come.

Telemakos

Old king. I am Telemakos. Father is Odysseus. You know Odysseus — you fight together at Troy. Father not come home. Long time. Very long time. Telemakos want know: you hear anything? Father alive? Father dead? Tell Telemakos true thing. Not soft thing. True thing.

Nestor

Ah.

(long pause)

Ah. You look like him. Same face. Same way of talking. Good.

* * *
Nestor begins long story, cannot stop

Okay. Nestor tell what Nestor know. Troy: very long war. Ten year. Many men die. Finally Troy fall. Greeks go home. But — problem. Greeks fight each other after. Agamemnon want stay. Menelaos want go. Big argument. Half men go one way. Half men go other way.

Nestor go one way. Odysseus start go same way as Nestor. Then Odysseus turn boat around. Go back to Agamemnon. After that: Nestor not know. Nestor come home. Nestor not see Odysseus again.

Telemakos

Father just... turned boat around?

Nestor

Yes. Why: Nestor not know.

Nestor tell you other thing. Important thing. You hear about Agamemnon?

Telemakos

Big king. Yes.

Nestor

Agamemnon come home. Wife's boyfriend waiting. Boyfriend kill Agamemnon. Very bad. But — Agamemnon had son. Son named Orestes. Orestes grow up. Orestes kill boyfriend. Orestes avenge father.

Nestor looks at Telemakos very directly.
Nestor

Good son do that.

Telemakos gets the point

...Yes.

* * *
Nestor

Nestor not know where Odysseus is. Sorry. But — Menelaos know more. Menelaos went different way home. Very long way. Egypt. Much trouble. But Menelaos home now. Sparta. Go ask Menelaos.

Nestor give Telemakos son. Peisistratos go with you. Good boy. Strong. Knows road to Sparta.

Peisistratos

Yes. I go. Is fine.

* * *
That night: big sacrifice. Everyone standing around the fire. Athena is there, looking like Mentor. Then — she changes. Big wings. She is an eagle. She flies straight up into the dark sky. Gone.
Everyone stares.
Nestor very quietly

...That was a god.

(long silence)

That was a god walking with you the whole time and nobody say anything.

He turns to Telemakos.
Nestor

Telemakos. The gods like you very much. This is good sign. Very good sign.

He turns to everyone else.
Nestor

Somebody get more bulls. We sacrifice more bulls. Right now. Tonight.

Peisistratos quietly, to Telemakos

Does this happen to you a lot?

Telemakos

No. First time.

Morning. Nestor gives gifts. Peisistratos brings the chariot. They leave for Sparta. Nestor watches from the shore for a long time.
Caveman Odyssey — after Homer — Ralph Hutchison
