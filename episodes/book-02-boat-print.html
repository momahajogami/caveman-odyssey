Caveman Odyssey: Boy Load Boat

Book II, Part 3 — a puppet script — after Homer

Puppets Needed

  • TELEMAKOS — quiet, determined; fewer words than usual
  • ATHENA / MENTOR — businesslike, already three steps ahead
  • EURYKLEIA — old nurse, worried, grumbling; loves him fiercely
  • CREW — silent background puppets loading the boat
Scene 1 — The Beach
After the failed assembly. Telemakos alone on the beach. Athena appears as Mentor.
ATHENA / MENTORTelemakos. Forget the old men. We get our own boat. I find crew. You go home. Get supplies. Wine. Barley. Oil. Strong jars. I come get you tonight. We go before sunrise.
TELEMAKOSTonight?
ATHENA / MENTORTonight. Suitors cannot know. Go.
Scene 2 — The Storeroom
The big storeroom. Jars everywhere. Telemakos pulling things down. Eurykleia comes in.
EURYKLEIAWhat is this. What are you doing. Where are you going.
TELEMAKOSI go to Pylos. I go to Sparta. I look for news of father.
EURYKLEIANo! No no no. Sea is dangerous! Father gone on sea! You want to be gone too? Your mother will cry. I will cry. Do not go.
TELEMAKOSI am going. Do not tell mother. Not until I am ten days gone. She will worry less if she cannot stop me.
Eurykleia stares at him. Then she starts filling jars. She grumbles the whole time. But she fills them.
Scene 3 — The Night Departure
Dark. The suitors are asleep somewhere else. Athena/Mentor on the beach with a fast black ship and twenty young men. Telemakos and crew carry everything down in silence.
Athena puts a wind in the sail. The ship moves.
Telemakos stands at the front of the boat, looking forward. He does not look back.

Puppet Show Tips

  • Eurykleia is the emotional center of this episode. She is furious and terrified and proud all at once. Let her fill the storeroom scene.
  • Her grumbling while filling the jars is a good moment for improvisation — the audience can feel her love in the grumbling.
  • The loading scene should be slow and physical — miming the weight of jars builds atmosphere. Keep it wordless.
  • The departure: dim the lights if you can. The ship moving = slow puppet drift across the stage. Silence.
  • "He does not look back." — hold this. It is the end of his childhood.
