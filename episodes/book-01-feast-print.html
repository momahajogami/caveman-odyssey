Caveman Odyssey: The Stranger at the Door

Book I, Part 2 — a puppet script — after Homer

Puppets Needed

  • TELEMAKOS — young man, sad at first, gets stronger
  • ATHENA / MENTES — disguised as an old sea captain; same puppet as Mentor from Book II
  • ANTINOOS — lead suitor, loud, comfortable, rude
  • PENELOPE — Telemakos's mother, sad, dignified
  • PHEMIOS — the bard; can be offstage voice or simple puppet with small instrument
  • SUITORS — two or three extras, eating and being loud in the background
Scene 1 — The Big House
Ithaca. The house of Odysseus. Suitors eating, drinking, loud. Telemakos sits alone, sad. A stranger appears at the door. Telemakos jumps up and runs over. The suitors do not move.
TELEMAKOSStranger! Welcome! Come in! Eat! Rest! We talk after.
They move away from the suitors. Sit together. Food comes.
ATHENA / MENTESThank you. I am Mentes. Old friend of your father. I come visit Odysseus. But — I see house full of strangers. Loud strangers. Eating everything. What happen here?
TELEMAKOSFather not come home from Troy. Long time now. These men want marry mother. Take father house. They stay and stay. They not go.
ATHENA / MENTESYour father is not dead. I know this. Odysseus stuck somewhere far away. But coming home. Maybe slow. But coming.
TELEMAKOSHow you know this?
ATHENA / MENTESI know. Listen. You need do two things. One: tomorrow, call assembly. Tell these men: go home. Say it loud. Two: get boat. Go find news of father. Ask king Nestor. Ask king Menelaos. They were at Troy. They know things.
Scene 2 — The Song
Phemios sings a sad song about the Greeks coming home from Troy. Penelope comes downstairs, crying.
PENELOPEPhemios. Please. Not that song. Too sad. Sing something else.
Room goes quiet. Everyone looks. Then —
TELEMAKOSMother. Go upstairs. This is men's business. Phemios sings what he wants. I will handle things here.
Penelope stares at her son. Something is different. She goes upstairs without arguing.
ANTINOOS(laughing, but not quite comfortable) Ha. Little boy talking big. Very funny.
Evening. Suitors leave. Stranger says goodbye and goes. Telemakos alone in the quiet hall. Then he goes to bed. He has a plan.

Puppet Show Tips

  • The suitors should be noisy background puppets during Scene 1 — thumping, grabbing food, ignoring everyone. Then go still when Telemakos speaks to Penelope.
  • Telemakos running to greet the stranger is the first important moment — contrast it sharply with the suitors who don't move.
  • The Penelope scene: Telemakos surprises himself. Play a beat of silence before his line — he doesn't plan it, it just comes out.
  • Antinoos's laugh should trail off. The audience should feel that something shifted, even if Antinoos won't admit it.
  • End quietly. Telemakos alone. Lights low if possible. He goes to bed different than he woke up.
