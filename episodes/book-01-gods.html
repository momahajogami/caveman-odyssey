The Gods Argue

Book I, Part 1 — On Olympos, the gods speak.

Zeus king of gods

Strange thing. Mortals always blame gods. They say: gods make bad things happen. But no. Mortals make bad things happen themselves.

Look at Aigisthos. We told him: do not touch Agamemnon wife. Do not kill Agamemnon. Bad things come. He did not listen. He touched wife. He killed Agamemnon. Now Orestes kill Aigisthos. Aigisthos choose this. Gods did not choose this.

Athena goddess of wisdom

Yes, Father. Aigisthos choose bad thing. Aigisthos deserve what he got. But — Odysseus. Odysseus choose nothing bad. Odysseus suffers anyway. Odysseus is good man. Odysseus pray to you. Odysseus give gifts. Why do you forget him? Why do you let him sit on island, crying, wanting home, and do nothing?

Zeus

I do not forget Odysseus. Odysseus is best of mortal men. But Poseidon — Poseidon does not forget either. Odysseus blind Poseidon son. Big son. One eye. Cyclops. Poseidon is god of sea. Odysseus must cross sea to get home. Every time Odysseus comes close, Poseidon pushes him back. Poseidon does not kill him. Just — keeps him away.

Athena

Poseidon is not here. Poseidon is far away, at feast with Aithiopians. He cannot see what we decide. Let us decide now — let Odysseus go home. Send Hermes to island of Kalypso. Tell Kalypso: let him go. I will go to Ithaka. I will find son of Odysseus — Telemakos. I will make him brave. I will send him to ask other kings: is father alive? Is father dead? And I will put fear in hearts of suitors eating father food in father house.

Zeus

Good. This is what I want too. Go. Do what you say. Odysseus will come home. Poseidon will be angry. But Poseidon cannot fight all gods at once. Odysseus comes home.

Caveman Odyssey — after Homer — Ralph Hutchison
