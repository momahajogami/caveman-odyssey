The Gods Argue

Book I, Part 1 — On a mountain far away, the gods decide what to do.

Very tall mountain. Clouds. Zeus sitting on big rock, eating. Thinking. Poseidon not here — Poseidon far away, at a party. Athena walks up.
Athena

Father. We talk about Odysseus?

Zeus king of gods, mouth full

Odysseus. Yes. Odysseus stuck. Island. Woman keep him. Name: Calypso. Calypso very pretty. Odysseus want go home. Calypso not let go.

Athena

Odysseus good man. Odysseus pray to us. Odysseus give gifts. We help other men come home from Troy. We not help Odysseus. Why not?

Zeus

Poseidon. Poseidon very mad at Odysseus. Odysseus hurt Poseidon son. Big son. One eye. Cyclops. Poseidon make sea very hard for Odysseus.

Athena

But Poseidon not here right now. Poseidon at party. Very far away. We decide now, before Poseidon come back. Send Odysseus home.

Zeus considers, then nods

Fine. Yes. Good. We send Hermes to island. Hermes tell Calypso: time to let him go. Odysseus go home on raft. Poseidon will be angry. But Odysseus get home eventually.

You — go to Ithaca. Help the boy. Boy needs to grow up. Boy needs to look for father. Make him brave.

Athena

Yes. I go now.

Athena jumps off mountain. Flies very fast. Down and down, toward the sea, toward a small island called Ithaca.
Caveman Odyssey — after Homer — Ralph Hutchison
