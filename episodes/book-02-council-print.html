Caveman Odyssey: The Island Council

Book II — a puppet script — after Homer

Puppets Needed

  • TELEMAKOS — young man, nervous, gets braver
  • ANTINOOS — big suitor, very pleased with himself
  • EURYMAKHOS — another suitor, agrees with Antinoos
  • MENTOR — old man, loyal, frustrated
  • LEOKRITUS — suitor, waves everyone off
  • HALITHERSES — very old man, reads bird omens
  • ATHENA — goddess disguised as Mentor (same puppet, different voice)
  • TWO EAGLES — paper or cloth, swooped overhead on sticks
Scene 1 — The Assembly
TELEMAKOS Men. Sit. Listen.
Father gone. Long time. Father not dead. Father not home. Bad men come to house. Bad men eat food. All food. Every day food. Telemakos say go. Bad men not go.
Men here! Old men! Do something!
Telemakos sits. Cries a little.
ANTINOOS Telemakos, no. Not our fault. Listen.
Mother trick. Mother say: wait. Cloth finish, then I pick husband. Day: make cloth. Night: untake cloth. Three year! We wait three year! Mother bad.
Send mother back to father-man. Father-man pick husband. Then we go. Simple.
TELEMAKOS No. You go. You go from house. Gods watching.
EURYMAKHOS Telemakos wrong. Antinoos right. Assembly over.
TELEMAKOS Fine. One thing. Give boat. Give twenty man. Telemakos go find father.
MENTOR Men! Shame! Big shame on all of you! Odysseus good king! Now son ask help. Nobody help. Bad!
Mentor sits, grumbling.
LEOKRITUS Mentor old. Mentor wrong. Assembly over. Telemakos: good luck with boat.
Leokritus laughs. Everyone starts to leave.
Scene 2 — The Eagles
Two eagles fly over fast (swoop puppets across). They scratch men below. They fly off.
HALITHERSES Wait! Eagles! Eagles mean Odysseus! Odysseus come back! Soon! Suitor men — very bad thing happen to you!
EURYMAKHOS Old man! Bird just bird. Sit down. We keep eating.
Assembly over. Empty stage.
Scene 3 — The Beach
Telemakos alone. Sits by water. Head down.
TELEMAKOS Gods. Help.
Stranger appears. Looks like Mentor. But eyes very bright.
ATHENA Telemakos. Why sit? Strong man not sit. Strong man go find.
TELEMAKOS Need boat. Nobody give boat. Suitor men very many. Telemakos small.
ATHENA Boat coming. Suitor men not as scary as they look. Trust me. Go home now. Pack food. Pack warm thing. Come back tonight. We go.
Telemakos stands straighter. Walks off. Stranger is gone. Just gone.

Puppet Show Tips

  • ANTINOOS and EURYMAKHOS can be the same puppet with a different hat.
  • The eagles work well as hand-shaped cloth on a stick — swoop fast, scratch a few heads, exit quickly.
  • ATHENA/MENTOR: same puppet, but when she is Athena, move more slowly and speak lower. Let the audience feel the difference.
  • Pause after "Gods. Help." — it is the quietest moment in the show. Let it land.
  • LEOKRITUS laughing is a good audience moment. Drag it out a beat.
